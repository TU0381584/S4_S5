Each controller against each criterion. Every cell prints its measured value. Shading is the signed distance to the row's target, divided by the largest distance in that row, running from darkest for furthest below target to lightest for furthest above. Targets are 90% compliance, half a control period of URLLC delay, no growth in parameters, and the tuned rule's reward per step in Env B at 19 gNBs. Hatched cells were not measured, a cross marks a controller that cannot run at another size, and private training is measured for AXIOM only.
